# 行为准则

## 我们的承诺

我们希望 Craft Agents 的交流环境保持友好、尊重、开放，并欢迎不同背景、经验和观点的人参与。

## 我们期待的行为

- 尊重他人，保持礼貌和善意。
- 欢迎新参与者，愿意帮助别人理解项目。
- 接受建设性的反馈，也用建设性的方式提出反馈。
- 聚焦问题本身，避免人身攻击、嘲讽或骚扰。
- 在讨论技术分歧时，优先说明事实、权衡和影响。

## 不可接受的行为

- 侮辱、骚扰、威胁或歧视。
- 发布他人隐私信息。
- 持续打断、挑衅或破坏正常讨论。
- 任何让参与者感到不安全或不受尊重的行为。

## 处理方式

如果你发现不合适的行为，可以通过 issue、私下联系维护者，或使用仓库中提供的安全/联系渠道反馈。维护者会尽量公平、及时地处理。

## 说明

本准则参考了 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 的精神，并按本仓库的个人维护场景做了简化。

# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Craft Agents to remain a friendly, respectful, open environment that welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and kind.
- Welcome newcomers and help others understand the project.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give feedback constructively.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- When discussing technical disagreements, explain facts, tradeoffs, and impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discrimination.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly disrupting or derailing normal discussion.
- Any behavior that makes participants feel unsafe or disrespected.

## Enforcement

If you notice unacceptable behavior, you may report it through an issue, contact a maintainer privately, or use the security/contact channel provided by the repository. Maintainers will try to handle reports fairly and promptly.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is inspired by the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and simplified for this personally maintained repository.
